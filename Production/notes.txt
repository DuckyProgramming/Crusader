The 7th Bersaglieri Regiment had only two battalions, the 10th and 11th. The 8th was disbanded in September due to losses.

Hollmann commanded the 3rd Battalion, 155th Infantry Regiment (not in game) that was near the 3rd Battalions of the 255th and 347th Regiments.

Zintel took control of the 115th Infantry Regiment not long before crusader began. I was unable to find any evidence that Marcks commanded it during this time period.

Finke and Warrelmann have both been claimed as the commander of 2nd Machine Gun Battalion. Based on my sources I believe Warrelmann was the commander at this time and Finke commanded 2nd Battalion, 115th Infantry Regiment instead.
