8:26 The 7th Armoured Brigade wasn't in is operation. The brigade had been nearly destroyed previously and had given up its remaining tanks to the other two brigades.
10:51 The Free French Brigade at Bir Hacheim wasn't destroyed until much later in the battle. Only the 150th Brigade had been destroyed at this point and some of the minefields cleared.
11:02 The 9th Indian Infantry Brigade (shown in Tobruk) was part of the force used for the attack (Operation Aberdeen). It also took high losses during this operation and retreated afterward, so it wasn't captured in Tobruk as shown in the video.
11:38 The South African troops on the coast did not retreat in the same direction as the British 50th Division to the south. They escaped along the coast, through Tobruk, instead.
13:43 Only 50% of the Italian 60th "Sabratha" Division was overrun and the rest of it was able to counterattack and restore the situation. The division was disbanded by Rommel after the battle, not destroyed by the attack.
14:57 The 185th "Folgore" Division had started off as a parachute division, but the symbol on the map is wrong as it had been converted to a regular infantry division by this point.

The research could've been better and I also dislike the perpetuation of some tropes, namely the obsession with tanks and the refusal to admit the times when Italian troops did show promise.
