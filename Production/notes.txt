Sources are murky on this but here's the path of the infantry of the three divisions:
Rommel arrives in Africa with IR zbV 200 with 2 machine gun battalions.
He is then joined by 15th Panzer with 15th Infantry Brigade with 2 Regiments, 104 and 115, each with 2 motorized infantry battalions (NOT machine gun).
These 6 battalions were then evenly distributed between 15th and 21st Panzer (renamed from 5th Light), with each receving 2 motorized infantry and 1 machine gun battalion in their regiments (115 and 104 respectively).
The loose 200th Regimental Staff would command random units during crusader.
At this time, 5 random 3rd Battalions were transferred to Africa to form 90th Light Division. They were joined by the 361st Light Regiment. 3 of the random battalions later formed 155th Infantry Regiment.
After Operation Crusader, the 3/255 Battalion was destroyed and disbanded. The remaining loose 3/347 Battalion and 1 from IR 155 became part of the new IR 200 which became a permanent part of 90th Light Division.
The panzer divisions still had 115th and 104th regiments, though in a greatly weakened state, with 1/104 lost with Major Bach at the frontier.
It appears the remaining machine gun battalions became regular infantry and the 15th Motorcycle Battalion was also amalgamated.
