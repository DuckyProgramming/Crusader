The 559th Anti-Tank Battalion is a unique unit. It appears in the original plans for Sperrverband Libyen, but never again.
It was originally formed to fight in France, but rather than head to Africa, it was sent to join Operation Barbarossa.
It was equipped with three companies of pretty regular anti-tank guns. It would later become self-propelled and fight in numerous battles.
