The very existence of this unit is questionable. Rather, it seems to be a very poor misinterpretation of Kampfgruppe Stumpfeld.
Rather than Scmid and Stelle "Infantry Regiments", this was actually KG Selle and KG Schidt. Herbert Selle was the 6th Army's engineer commander.
Saddnebruck also seems to be a typo for Saddenbruck or even Sauerbruck, if those are the same person.

It was also probably not formed out of Soviet deserters but by alarm, rear services, and stragglers from the 6th Army.
The only possible Ost unit there was a supposed "Est Schuma Battalion 36 'Arensburg'" which I have no information about.
The Kharkov and Morossovskaya battalions were not formed from Russians or Cossacks either, but by Germans in the service units in those areas.
The Morossovskaya/Morrosowskaja Battalion may also be named the II rather than the IV.
More at: https://forum.axishistory.com/viewtopic.php?t=82773, https://www.feldgrau.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=7123 (Abrendroth), https://www.feldgrau.net/forum/viewtopic.php?t=8220&start=30
The originally document that the above was misinterpreted from can also be found, at: https://forum.axishistory.com/download/file.php?id=393335&t=1.
