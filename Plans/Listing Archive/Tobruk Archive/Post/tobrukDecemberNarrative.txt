Order of battle for the garrison:

The Guardia alla Frontiera is responsible for the existing garrison. The 31st and 32nd Coverage Sectors were initially in the city, but the 32nd was removed along with some of its personnel to reconstitute other garrisons. Out of the initial 12 strongpoints in the garrison, only 8 remain:

31st Coverage Sector
    31st/A Subsector
        Strongpoint Sidi Daud (19 Fortifications)
        Strongpoint Suesi (6 Fortifications)
    31st/B Subsector
        Strongpoint Bir Junes (11 Fortifications)
        Strongpoint Bir Umm Haleiga (11 Fortifications)
Elements, 32nd Coverage Sector
    Strongpoint Hagfet Umm Haleiga (12 Fortifications)
    Strongpoint Bir el Medauuar  (10 Fortifications)
    Strongpoint Es Sehel (6 Fortifications)
    Strongpoint Umm Cuheil (18 Fortifications)

The fortifications that are manned each have 2 outdated Schwarzlose machine guns for a total of 186. However note that these can be supplemented by ones later found in storage. They are covered by thin defenses, consisting of mines, concertina wire, and in some places, a ditch.
Supporting these Guardia alla Frontiera elements is the 31st Mixed Engineer unit with a company strength of 115 men of various specializations. There's also the following additional infantry:

31st Libyan Coastal Battalion (3 rifle companies without any heavy weapons)
64th Replacement Battalion (Standard infantry organization of 3 rifle companies and 1 heavy company with 8 machine guns and 18 light mortars)
3rd Company, 62nd Replacement Battalion
63rd Garrison Company

While these units are somewhat more mobile than the static Guardia alla Frontiera strongpoints, the entire fortress suffers from fragile communications networks and a lack of any motor vehicles.

The machine guns can be deployed to support the outdated Schwarlose models in the fortifications or be used to support infantry. Note that the machine guns taken from aircraft suffer from poor performances when used by ground personnel due to cooling issues.
Note that the multipurpose guns of 75mm caliber or larger lack anti-tank ammunition. For the naval guns, personnel from the navy are available to crew them, while makeshift crews will be necessary for the rest. If necessary, about 160 personnel for various duties can be drawn from the depots and the Tobruk Carabinieri Station.
